Stranded at North Sentinel

A solo sailor's yacht dies at night and the current carries him onto the reef of the one island you must never land on. Then: what to actually do. 9:16, 80.5 s, every frame drawn in code, one camera that never stops.

Camera

One continuous move per beat, eased, plus a slow handheld drift so it never stops. Beats join with push-throughs that keep the camera's speed constant across the join, never static cuts. The subject stays on the vertical centre line.

The islanders

The Sentinelese are never shown. Their presence is only firelight in the trees and on the beach. No faces, no figures, no caricature.

Facts on screen

Mayday on VHF channel 16 and an emergency beacon; the zone is patrolled by the Indian Coast Guard; disease risk; MV Primrose aground on the reef in August 1981, crew stayed aboard and were airlifted out. Sources disagree on 3 days to a week, so the line says "for days".

Sound (after picture lock)

Music under the voice, 12–18 dB down: tense pulse, a hit on CRUNCH, a lift for the helicopter, warm dawn ending. Effects: engine sputter, clunk, waves, radio squelch, beacon beeps, rotor, switch click.
