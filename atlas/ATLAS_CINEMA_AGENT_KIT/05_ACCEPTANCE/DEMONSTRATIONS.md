# Three real demo production briefs

## A — Cinematic 1920s street, 9:16, 20 seconds

Dusk on a rainy European street; stone façades with period-correct set dressing; subtle window lights, cobblestone and puddle roughness; five motivated shots, slow dolly/side-tracking camera, haze, ambient sound and one narrated line. Real assets or procedural equivalents only; if historically accurate assets are absent, label approximation.

## B — Premium product hero, 16:9, 15 seconds

A reflective machined aluminum and glass object rotates on a premium black studio set. Three distinct shots: macro push, controlled orbit and pull-back reveal. Stripbox highlights, proper micro-roughness, physically plausible shadow/contact and deliberate tempo. Audio sting and clean closing slate. Inspect for fireflies/noise and texture issues.

## C — Vertical true-story explainer, 9:16, 45–60 seconds

Story outline with hook, escalation and payoff; environment changes as needed; narrator with word-aligned captions, 3D objects and 2D editorial elements from Remotion, nuanced camera movement and a restrained licensed SFX bed. Do not fabricate historical facts; if research isn't available use a fictional, clearly labeled storyline for the test.

## Demo evidence

For each: brief JSON, shot plan JSON, assets/license manifest, `.blend` or scene build script, preview contact sheet, render logs, actual frame folder, final MP4, ffprobe JSON, cost estimation vs actual and QC findings. Tests should fail on intentionally introduced missing asset and missing frame.
